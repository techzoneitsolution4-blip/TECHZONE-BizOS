# TECHZONE BizOS V2.0 — Module Register

## Platform foundation
- Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
- Environment configuration and Git workflow
- Loading, error and health-check handling

## Identity and security
- Authentication, sessions and OTP
- Tenant isolation, roles and permissions
- Row Level Security (RLS), rate limiting and audit logs

## Business core
- Business profiles, branches, staff and settings
- Customers, vendors, leads and communication history
- Categories, products and services

## Operations and finance
- Orders, bookings, quotations and invoices
- Purchases, payments and payment-provider adapters
- Inventory, stock movements, expenses, income and Khata
- Client and vendor transaction OTP, idempotency and audit trail

## Digital presence and communication
- Website templates, public business links, QR and SEO
- Notifications, email, SMS and WhatsApp integrations
- Webhook validation and delivery tracking

## Intelligence and growth
- AI knowledge base, receptionist and usage limits
- Reviews, loyalty, coupons, campaigns and opt-out
- Dashboard analytics, reports and exports

## SaaS administration
- Plans, subscriptions, add-ons and commissions
- Tenant lifecycle, platform revenue, support and audit

## Implementation rule
Folders are scaffolding, not completed features. Each module must
receive implementation, authorization, validation, tenant-isolation
tests, error/loading/empty states, documentation and a Git commit
before its roadmap step is marked DONE.
